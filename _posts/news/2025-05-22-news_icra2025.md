<p>
Members of the HARCO Lab participated in the <strong>IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA) 2025</strong>, held in <strong>Atlanta, USA</strong>.
</p>

<div style="display: flex; gap: 20px; justify-content: center; flex-wrap: wrap; margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em;">
{% include figure.html image="images/news/250522_icra2025/icra_group_1.jpg" width="50%" %}
</div>

<p>
On <strong>May 19th</strong>, <a href="/members/wansoo_kim">Prof. Wansoo Kim</a> organized the workshop titled:<br>
<em>"HOW DO ROBOTS CARE?"</em><br>
which focused on affective human–robot interaction and personalized robotic assistance.
During the session, <a href="/members/seongmin_ha">Seongmin Ha</a> gave a presentation entitled:<br>
<em>"Pelvic IMU-Based Gait Phase Classification for Hemiplegic Gait using Kernel-Embedded ELM: Validation on Healthy Participants."</em>
</p>

<div style="display: flex; gap: 20px; justify-content: center; flex-wrap: wrap; margin-top: 1em; margin-bottom: 1em;">

</div>

<p>
<a href="/members/donggyu_lee">Donggyu Lee</a> and <a href="/members/soonwoong_hwang">Soonwoong Hwang</a> co-authored the paper titled:<br>
<em>"Proactive Ergonomic Human Motion Generation for Human-Robot Collaboration,"</em><br>
which was presented by <a href="/members/wansoo_kim">Prof. Wansoo Kim</a> and <a href="/members/soonwoong_hwang">Soonwoong Hwang</a> during the conference. 
</p>

<p>
on <strong>May 22nd</strong>, <a href="/members/seungmin_choi">Seungmin Choi</a> presented his research at the main conference session. His talk was titled:<br>
<em>"A Multi-Task Energy-Aware Impedance Controller for Enhanced Safety in Physical Human-Robot Interaction."</em>
